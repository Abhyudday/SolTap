STEP 3: SETUP DEVICE 1 (MERCHANT)
================================================================================

1. Launch app
2. Select "MERCHANT" mode (permanent choice!)
3. Tap Settings icon (top right)
4. Tap "Copy Address"
5. Fund wallet:
   
   solana airdrop 1 <PASTE_ADDRESS> --url devnet
   
   Or web: https://faucet.solana.com (select Devnet)

6. Wait 10 seconds, tap "Refresh Balance"
7. Verify balance shows ~1.0 SOL
8. Go back to main screen

================================================================================
STEP 4: SETUP DEVICE 2 (BUYER)
================================================================================

1. Launch app
2. Select "BUYER" mode (permanent choice!)
3. Tap Settings icon (top right)
4. Tap "Copy Address"
5. Fund wallet:
   
   solana airdrop 0.5 <PASTE_ADDRESS> --url devnet

6. Wait 10 seconds, tap "Refresh Balance"
7. Verify balance shows ~0.5 SOL
8. Go back to main screen

================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

"No merchant detected"
  → Bring devices closer (<1 meter)
  → Ensure merchant shows "Advertising..."
  → Check Bluetooth is enabled
  → Restart both apps

"Payment failed: insufficient funds"
  → Fund buyer wallet more: solana airdrop 0.5 ADDRESS --url devnet
  → Check balance in Settings

"Permission denied"
  → Settings → Apps → Solana Tap Pay → Permissions
  → Enable: Location, Bluetooth, Notifications

"RPC error" or timeout
  → Check internet connection
  → Try again (devnet can be slow)

================================================================================
VERIFY TRANSACTION
================================================================================

Get signature from buyer success screen, then:

https://explorer.solana.com/tx/<SIGNATURE>?cluster=devnet

Should show:
  ✓ Status: Success
  ✓ From: Buyer address
  ✓ To: Merchant address
  ✓ Amount: ~0.05 SOL
